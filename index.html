Brand Origin

原野日常從一個簡單的信念出發：永續不應該是負擔，而是一種習慣。我們相信，真正好的東西不需要過度包裝，不需要促銷話術，只需要誠實地說清楚它從哪裡來、用什麼做的、能陪你走多遠。

Wildland Living was born from a simple belief — that sustainability shouldn't be a burden, but a habit. Good things don't need loud packaging or clever marketing. They just need to be honest about where they come from, what they're made of, and how long they'll last.
